Unmodified 1s always fail. Crits only apply to hit rolls and always succeed. On saves, the "fails" are the damage that goes through. Mortal crits deal the same damage per hit and ignore saves. The ward step rolls one die per point of damage, mortal damage included. Dice and damage boxes accept formulas like 20x2+1, D3 or 10xD6. Unit stats come from the community BSData project and may lag behind new rules.
